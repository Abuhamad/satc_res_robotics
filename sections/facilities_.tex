\section{Overview}

The research team at Loyola University Chicago contributes to all proposed thrusts.
Loyola also supports Thrusts 1 and 2 through dataset construction and scalable analysis via our own advanced resources and experience with obtaining allocations at leadership computing centers.

This work is housed in the \textbf{Software and Systems Laboratory (SSL)} within the Department of Computer Science. SSL is an active research group focused on systems, HPC, and secure software engineering. All infrastructure is maintained by Mr.~Miao Ye, our full-time systems and lab manager.

\section{Available Resources}

\subsection{Computing and Storage (Thrusts 1 \& 2)}

Loyola maintains departmental servers suitable for software repository mining, dataset analysis, and longitudinal studies in the Research Data Center, which is a shared space occupied primarily by Computer Science but also including resources from the Biology, Chemistry, Data Science, and Physics departments/programs at Loyola University Chicago. These systems provide compute capacity and durable storage and backup for open-source software analysis.

In addition, the PI has supercomputing allocations at Argonne National Laboratory, enabling access to leadership-class systems such as Polaris and Aurora, along with high-performance storage and data transfer facilities.
These resources allow the team to scale workloads that exceed local capacity.

\subsection{Education and Outreach (Thrust 1 \& 2)}

Loyola supports undergraduate research via the \textbf{LUROP} program, which allows students to engage in funded research with no additional grant cost. SSL also contributes to interdisciplinary outreach via university initiatives like the Center for Textual Studies and Digital Humanities.

\subsection{Web Hosting and Software Deployment (Thrusts 1 \& 2)}

The lab manages Linux-based servers that support full-stack web hosting, API services, and container-based deployment using Docker and Kubernetes. These are used to distribute research tools and publish datasets to the community.
