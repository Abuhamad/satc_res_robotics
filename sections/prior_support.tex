\BfPara{Award \#2335700: Education DCL: EAGER: Developing Experiential Cybersecurity and Privacy Training for AI Practitioners} (PI: Abuhamad, 11/01/2023 -- 10/31/2025, \$299,905.00). 
\textbf{Intellectual Merit:} The project establishes experiential cybersecurity training for AI professionals. The program includes a comprehensive 12-workshop experiential session. %The workshop series provides the knowledge and skills needed to build AI systems that are not only technically sound from an AI perspective, but also secure, ethical, and privacy-preserving. 
\textbf{Broader Impacts:}
The completion of the program will result in a workforce proficient in the use of AI with an understanding of its cybersecurity issues. %The program's materials and outcomes will be shared through various channels to promote responsible AI practices. 
Educational resources are available online \cite{secureai_website}. Findings were presented at CAE symposia and CISSE \cite{secureai_findings,secureai_findings_2}.

Scholars in this program engage in interdisciplinary research and education, which fosters critical thinking, independence, teamwork, and technical knowledge essential for a cybersecurity career. %culum emphasizes a hands-on approach to learning, enabling students to apply concepts and confidently utilize the latest tools.

 This initiative will benefit society by enhancing national security and protecting various critical sectors against cyber-attacks.

\BfPara{Award \#2343595: Collaborative Research: EAGER: CET: Exploring The Risks and Rewards of Large Language Models in Enabling Energy-Efficient Data Center Software Infrastructure} (PI: Thiruvathukal, 08/01/2024 -- 07/31/2027, \$147,947.00).
\textbf{Intellectual Merit}: The project applies large language models (LLMs) to automate energy-efficiency optimization of data center software, developing a catalog of energy-focused prompting/feedback strategies paired with a hierarchy of energy measures, and generating empirical evidence on how effective LLMs are at producing energy-efficient code.
\textbf{Broader Impacts}: Data centers consume \textasciitilde 2\% of U.S.\ energy and growing; more energy-efficient software reduces this footprint and supports national energy independence. %The project also develops systems-thinking-based educational materials on energy-efficient computing, disseminated via a public workshop.
\emph{Publications}: \cite{peng2026agents,peng2026sysllmatic}.

\BfPara{Award \#2537309: Collaborative Research: Planning: CROSS: Building a Community aROund Securing the Research Software Supply Chain} (PI: Thiruvathukal, 09/01/2025 -- 08/31/2027, \$49,996.00).
\textbf{Intellectual Merit}: A planning effort (\emph{CROSS}) to build foundational knowledge of Research Software Supply Chains (RSSCs) --- via a systematic literature review producing a conceptual model of RSSC security threats, empirical measurement of the security posture of real-world research software and its dependencies (with national-lab-provided datasets), and community workshops with research software engineers, synthesized using STAMP and TOE frameworks into a unified threat model.
\textbf{Broader Impacts}: Produces a roadmap for securing the research software supply chain, safeguarding scientific integrity and national security; engages undergraduate students in cybersecurity/RSE workforce development.
\emph{Publications}: none to date.
